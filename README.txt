KLS SameDay Office v16 — Customer Portal

INSTALL
1. Run KLS_SameDay_v16_Customer_Portal.sql in Supabase SQL Editor.
2. Replace app.js, styles.css, index.html, manifest.json, sw.js, build.js and package.json in GitHub.
3. Commit summary: Upgrade to v16 Customer Portal
4. Push and wait for Vercel Ready.
5. Hard refresh the website.

CUSTOMER ACCESS SETUP
1. The customer opens the website and creates an account using their email address.
2. In the office system open Settings > Customer Portal Access.
3. Choose the CRM customer and enter the same login email.
4. Press Enable Customer Portal.
5. When that customer signs in, they see only their portal.

FEATURES
- Secure customer-specific dashboard
- Customer booking requests
- Office approval converts requests into confirmed jobs
- Active and completed job history
- Live tracking links where available
- POD access where available
- Customer invoices and outstanding balances
- One-click rebooking requests
- Existing v15 functionality retained

NOTE
Online card payments and automatic email delivery are not included in v16. The portal displays invoices and account balances only.
