Driver Privacy & GPS Tracking Notice

Effective: 1 August 2026 · Last reviewed: 1 August 2026

This notice applies to employed drivers, workers, owner-drivers and subcontractors using the KLS Driver App.

Information used

We may use driver identity and contact details, vehicle and availability information, assigned jobs, job-stage timestamps, messages, incident reports, collection and delivery evidence, and location data recorded during active work.

When GPS operates

Live location recording starts when a driver begins an assigned job and is intended to stop when the job is completed. It must not be used for continuous private-life monitoring or to track a driver when they are not carrying out an active KLS job.

Why GPS is used

  • to dispatch and manage assigned work;
  • to provide accurate operational status and customer service;
  • to protect drivers, customers and goods;
  • to investigate delays, incidents, complaints or claims;
  • to demonstrate collection and delivery performance.

Lawful basis and proportionality

Location and workflow information is used where necessary to perform courier contracts and for KLS's legitimate interests in operating a safe, accountable delivery service. Access is limited to authorised KLS office users and relevant service providers.

Driver responsibilities

Drivers must protect their login, use only their own account, keep the device secure, photograph only relevant delivery evidence, avoid unnecessary personal information, and report loss, unauthorised access or incorrect records promptly.

Retention and rights

Detailed GPS information is normally retained for no more than 90 days. Other driver and delivery evidence follows the Data Retention Policy. Drivers may request access or correction and may raise concerns without disadvantage.
