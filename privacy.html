Customer Privacy Notice

Effective: 1 August 2026 · Last reviewed: 1 August 2026

This notice explains how Kings Logistics Services Ltd, trading as KLS SameDay, uses personal information when providing courier and logistics services.

Who we are

Kings Logistics Services Ltd (company number 16165066) is the controller of the personal information described in this notice. Our registered office is 171 Slade Road, Clacton-On-Sea, England, CO15 5EG.

Information we collect

  • customer, contact and recipient names;
  • email addresses, telephone numbers and collection or delivery addresses;
  • quotation, booking, job, invoice and payment records;
  • delivery progress, timestamps and limited vehicle-location information;
  • collection and delivery photographs, recipient signatures and POD notes;
  • communications, complaints and service enquiries.

Why we use it

We use this information to quote, arrange, perform and evidence deliveries; communicate service updates; invoice and collect payment; prevent fraud; resolve complaints or claims; maintain business records; and comply with legal obligations.

Lawful bases

We normally process information because it is necessary to perform a contract or take steps requested before a contract, to comply with legal obligations, or for our legitimate interests in operating, securing and evidencing a courier service. Where consent is required, it may be withdrawn.

Who receives information

Information may be shared only where necessary with assigned drivers or subcontractors, the booking customer, authorised recipients, insurers, professional advisers, regulators, law-enforcement bodies, and contracted technology providers including hosting, database and email providers.

Photographs and signatures

Drivers are instructed to photograph goods or the delivery location and to avoid faces, number plates and unrelated private information wherever reasonably possible. Signatures and recipient names are collected as delivery evidence.

International processing

Some contracted technology providers may process information outside the UK. Where this occurs, we require an appropriate UK data-transfer safeguard or another lawful transfer mechanism.

How long we keep it

Our standard periods are described in the Data Retention Policy. Relevant records may be retained longer where a complaint, insurance matter, tax enquiry or legal claim remains open.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or transfer of your personal information. You may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Security

We use account authentication, access restrictions, encrypted connections, database security rules, backups and limited role-based access. No internet service can be guaranteed completely risk-free.
