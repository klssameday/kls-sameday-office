Data Retention Policy

Effective: 1 August 2026 · Last reviewed: 1 August 2026

We keep personal information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for delivery operations, accounting, legal obligations, security and the handling of claims.

Standard retention periods

  • Invoices, payments and essential accounting records: six years from the end of the relevant company financial year.
  • Essential job record and basic POD certificate: six years, supporting accounting records and possible contractual claims.
  • Collection and delivery photographs: two years after job completion.
  • Recipient signature image: two years after job completion.
  • Detailed GPS/location data: 90 days after job completion.
  • Unsuccessful quote requests: 12 months after the last contact.
  • Customer and driver accounts: while active, followed by review after two years of inactivity.
  • Routine service emails and messages: two years unless attached to an accounting record, complaint or claim.

Legal holds and exceptions

Relevant records are not deleted while a complaint, unpaid debt, insurance matter, tax enquiry, safeguarding concern, fraud investigation or legal claim is active. Once the matter is resolved, the normal period resumes or the record is securely deleted when no longer required.

Deletion and anonymisation

When a period expires, personal information is deleted, anonymised or reduced to the minimum financial record that must still be retained. Backups expire through their normal protected rotation.

Reviews

KLS reviews due records periodically and reviews this policy at least annually or after a material change to its systems or legal requirements.
